# Taste — Design Quality Gate

Taste is a reviewer, not a generator. Apply to every meaningful design-ui, motion-ui, 3d-ui or design-system implementation. For music videos, edits and genre-driven visual direction, load [the existing video direction workflow](references/video-direction.md); do not impose its genre palette on product interfaces. Its reference to the genre taxonomy resolves to [genre-taxonomy.md](references/genre-taxonomy.md) here.

## Review rendered evidence

Inspect the actual product brief, existing design language and rendered result at relevant desktop and mobile widths. Judge hierarchy and interaction in real loading, populated, empty and error states. Compare with the intended direction, not a universal aesthetic preset. When rendering is unavailable, report the gate as unverified; do not fabricate a pass.

Reject unjustified patterns: rounded card everything; cards inside cards; pill everything; purple/blue gradients everywhere; meaningless glow; random glassmorphism; floating blobs; fake dashboards; fake metrics; huge generic marketing headlines; massive radius everywhere; random sparkles; everything centered; excessive empty whitespace; generic feature grids; random icons; meaningless hover scaling; arbitrary animation; decorative Three.js; gray-on-gray low contrast. Identify the concrete element and the harm instead of keyword policing a justified component.

Reward deliberate typography, visual hierarchy, rhythm, restraint, product identity, meaningful density, strong and responsive composition, intentional interaction, appropriate whitespace, coherent component grammar, purposeful motion, clarity and accessibility.

## Gate and revision

1. Record observed evidence, severity and a concrete fix for each failure. Check alignment with screen purpose; a settings page and an immersive product story need different intensity.
2. If the UI fails, automatically revise the design in the authorized scope. Return to design-ui or the responsible design workflow; do not merely report failure and stop.
3. Re-render the revised UI and repeat the review. Do not silently lower standards to obtain a pass. If blocked by missing assets, runtime or user decisions, state the precise limitation and leave the gate unverified.
4. After passing, verify responsive composition, keyboard and focus behavior, contrast, labels, reduced motion and final visual output. Preserve functional correctness during revisions.
5. Report pass/fail/unverified, screens and states inspected, defects corrected and any remaining limitation. An unsupported visual claim is itself a failed verification.
